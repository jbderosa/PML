Movie night shortlist

Distinctive movies with a strong point of view.

A mix of dark comedy, crime, formal experimentation, eccentric characters, sharp dialogue and unmistakable director/writer voices—mostly from the late ’90s and early ’00s.

Rotten Tomatoes scores checked September 28, 2026. Scores can change as reviews and ratings are added.

The shortlist

Tomatometer = critics · Popcornmeter = audience

O Brother, Where Art Thou?

2000 · Coen brothers · comedy / adventure

A Depression-era odyssey with deadpan absurdity, folk music, mythic structure and George Clooney at maximum comic vanity.

78% critics89% audience

The Aviator

2004 · Martin Scorsese · biographical drama

A large-scale Howard Hughes portrait: aviation, Hollywood, ambition, obsession and a deliberately escalating sense of confinement.

86% critics79% audience
TrailerRotten Tomatoes

Rushmore

1998 · Wes Anderson · comedy

Precise, melancholy and very funny: an overcommitted teenager, a weary industrialist and a rivalry neither is emotionally equipped to handle.

Boogie Nights

1997 · Paul Thomas Anderson · drama

An exuberant rise-and-fall ensemble film about an improvised family, fame and the adult-film business in the late ’70s and early ’80s.

91% critics89% audience
TrailerRotten Tomatoes

Memento

2000 · Christopher Nolan · mystery / thriller

A revenge mystery built around fractured memory; the structure makes the audience share the protagonist’s uncertainty instead of merely observing it.

94% critics94% audience
TrailerRotten Tomatoes

Being John Malkovich

1999 · Spike Jonze / Charlie Kaufman · surreal comedy

A wildly original metaphysical comedy about identity, desire and control—beginning with a portal into John Malkovich’s head and getting stranger from there.

An atmospheric underground-poker movie whose audience reputation substantially exceeds its original critical reception.

Bill & Ted’s Excellent Adventure

The outlier here: a cheerful, absurd time-travel comedy powered by total commitment to its gloriously uncomplicated protagonists.
